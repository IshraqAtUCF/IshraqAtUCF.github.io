About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Engineering at the University of Central Florida, advised by Dr. Sazadur Rahman, and received my M.S. in Computer Engineering from UCF in August 2026. I am a UCF Trustee's Doctoral Fellow and a Future Faculty Laureate (FFL).

My work focuses on practical, automation-driven security assurance for semiconductor designs: AI-assisted threat modeling and vulnerability reasoning, automated security property generation, and formal verification. I also work on keeping security intent intact through synthesis and physical design, and on authentication and trust for chiplet-based and System-in-Package systems.

From May to September 2026, I was an XPU Security Research Intern at Intel Corporation in Folsom, CA, working on security research for next-generation XPU (accelerator) architectures.

Before my Ph.D., I worked as a Hardware Design & Verification Engineer on custom RISC-V processors for XCelerium, an Irvine, CA start-up, through Dynamic Solution Innovators.

Current Focus

  • SecureOpenROAD: security assurance for open-source synthesis and physical design
