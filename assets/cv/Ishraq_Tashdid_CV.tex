% ─── RESEARCH EXPERIENCE ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section*{Research Experience}
\entry{\textbf{Graduate Research Assistant} --- University of Central Florida, Orlando, FL}{Jan.\ 2024--Present}
\subnote{Hardware Security and Trustworthy Computing Lab; advised by Dr.\ Sazadur Rahman}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{AI-assisted hardware security verification and vulnerability reasoning.}
  Developed methods connecting standardized vulnerability knowledge, hardware structural
  information, AI-assisted reasoning, and formal/property-based verification to automate security
  analysis of semiconductor designs. This research includes ATLAS and continuing work on Aegis and
  related security-verification frameworks.
  \item \textbf{ATLAS --- threat-to-assertion learning for SoC security.}
  Developed an AI-assisted framework that identifies hardware assets, maps relevant weaknesses from
  standardized sources such as CWE, constructs structured threat models, and generates
  assertion-based security properties and formal-verification scripts. Across three HACK@DAC
  benchmark designs, ATLAS identified 39 of 48 evaluated weaknesses and generated correct security
  properties for 33 of the 39 identified cases.
  \item \textbf{Security knowledge across synthesis and implementation.}
  Investigating how security assets, assumptions, countermeasures, verification obligations, and
  policies can be preserved and checked across synthesis transformations. Secure-SDC focuses on
  retaining security knowledge and verification obligations across synthesis.
  \item \textbf{Security assurance for open-source semiconductor design flows.}
  Since Sept.\ 2026, contributing to SecureOpenROAD research on security assurance across synthesis
  and physical implementation. Identified security-critical metrics for semiconductor design flows
  that helped shape the methodology in the submitted research proposal.
  \item \textbf{Security of heterogeneous and chiplet-based systems.}
  Developed authentication and trust architectures for System-in-Package and chiplet-based systems,
  including SAFE-SiP, AuthenTree, InterPUF, and RoutePUF. The work combines multi-party computation,
  physically unclonable functions, distributed trust, and interposer-resident security mechanisms.
  \item \textbf{Secure test and verification for heterogeneous systems.}
  Investigating security-aware test and verification for chiplet-based systems, including protection
  of authentication, test, and inter-chiplet information in multi-vendor environments.
  \item \textbf{Hardware IP protection and reconfigurable security.}
  Developed ECOLogic, an eFPGA-augmented SoC methodology supporting post-fabrication adaptability,
  logic obfuscation, and hardware IP protection while maintaining practical implementation
  characteristics.
  \item \textbf{Machine learning for semiconductor physical design.}
  Developed BeyondPPA, a human-inspired reinforcement-learning methodology for macro placement that
  incorporates practical physical-design considerations and post-route reliability objectives beyond
  conventional PPA optimization. The work was selected as a Best Paper Award Nominee at MLCAD 2025.
\end{itemize}
